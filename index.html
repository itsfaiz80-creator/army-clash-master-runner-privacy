Privacy Policy

Army Clash Master Runner, by GameFai. Last updated: November 7, 2026.

This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Army Clash Master Runner on Android (package name com.gamefai.armyclashmasterrunner). "We" means GameFai, the developer of the game.

The short version

  • You do not need an account, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your game progress stays on your phone. We do not run a server that receives it.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google and its ad partners collect some device data to do that.
  • If you subscribe to No Ads, Google Play handles the payment. We never see your card details.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your level, coins, stars, upgrades, sound and graphics settings, ad timing counters and subscription status in a file on your phone. This file is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it.

Advertising

The free version of the game shows banner, full-screen, app-open and optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google. When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's Advertising ID, and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data from the SDK.

Google uses this data to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud, and may use it for personalized ads where allowed. The game does not ask for GPS location. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent for ads

Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your choice about personalized ads before ads are requested. When this applies, you can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings screen, under Privacy Choices.

Purchases

The No Ads subscription is sold through Google Play. Google processes your payment and tells the game only whether your subscription is active. We do not receive your payment details. You can cancel at any time in your Google Play subscriptions. Google's help page explains how.

Unity

The game is built with the Unity engine and uses Unity's in-app purchasing software. These components may process basic technical information, such as device model and operating system, so the game runs correctly. See the Unity privacy policy for game players. We have not enabled Unity Analytics or Unity Ads in this game.

Permissions the game uses

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and reach Google Play.
  • Advertising ID: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for ads.
  • Google Play Billing: for the No Ads subscription.

Your choices

  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your Android settings, under Privacy, then Ads. The exact path depends on your phone.
  • Fewer ads: the No Ads subscription removes banner, between-game and app-open ads. Rewarded ads are always optional.
  • Your local data: uninstall the game, or clear its data in Android settings.
  • Data held by Google: manage it through your Google Account.

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or CCPA may give you further rights over your personal data. We do not hold personal data about you on our own systems, so requests about ad data are best sent to Google. You may still contact us with any question.

Sharing and security

We do not sell your personal information, because we do not collect it. Data is shared only as described above, directly between your device and Google or Unity, using encrypted connections.

Children

This game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help you with it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
